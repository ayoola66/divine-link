## Description (4,000 characters max)

```
Divine Link revolutionises how churches display scripture during sermons. Using advanced speech recognition, it listens to your pastor speak and automatically detects Bible verse references—displaying them on screen the moment they're mentioned.

NO MORE MANUAL SEARCHING
Gone are the days of frantically searching for verses whilst the pastor waits. Divine Link does the work for you, detecting references like "John 3:16" or "First Corinthians chapter 13" and instantly displaying the full text.

SEAMLESS PROPRESENTER INTEGRATION
Divine Link connects directly to ProPresenter, your church's presentation software. When a verse is detected, it's automatically pushed to your stage display or audience screens. Your media team can focus on worship, not verse hunting.

INTELLIGENT DETECTION
• Recognises spoken verse references in multiple formats
• Handles "chapter and verse" or "verse only" references
• Understands numbered books (1 Timothy, 2 Kings, etc.)
• Detects verse ranges (John 3:16-18)
• Confidence indicator shows detection reliability

MULTIPLE BIBLE TRANSLATIONS
Choose from built-in translations including:
• King James Version (KJV)
• American Standard Version (ASV)
• World English Bible (WEB)

PERFECT FOR YOUR TEAM
• Pastor Profiles: Save speech patterns for improved accuracy
• Service Sessions: Track all verses mentioned during each service
• History Browser: Review past services and their scriptures
• Panic Button: Instantly clear the screen with one click (F12)

PROFESSIONAL AUDIO SUPPORT
• Works with any microphone or audio interface
• BlackHole support for capturing system audio
• Real-time audio level monitoring
• Compatible with USB mixers and professional setups

BUILT FOR RELIABILITY
Divine Link runs locally on your Mac—no internet required during services. Your scripture detection works even when connectivity is unreliable.

DESIGNED FOR MACOS
A native macOS application built with SwiftUI, offering the performance and integration you expect from Apple software. Menu bar access, keyboard shortcuts, and a clean, intuitive interface.

Whether you're a small church plant or a large congregation, Divine Link helps your congregation engage with God's Word by putting scripture on screen exactly when it's spoken.

Download Divine Link and transform how your church experiences scripture during worship.
```
