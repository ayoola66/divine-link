# Story 9.1: Brand Identity & Asset Kit

## Status
Draft

## Story

**As a** Divine Link product owner and marketing lead,
**I want** a comprehensive, consistent brand identity with ready-to-use asset templates,
**so that** all future marketing content looks professional, cohesive, and trustworthy — and can be produced quickly without starting from scratch each time.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. A brand style guide document exists covering: primary/secondary colours (with hex codes), typography (Cinzel for display, DM Sans for body), tone of voice, logo usage rules, and spacing guidelines.
2. Social media profile images (400×400px) are created and saved for all six target platforms: YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, and X/Twitter.
3. Social media channel banner/header images are created for: YouTube (2560×1440px), Facebook (851×315px), LinkedIn (1584×396px), and X/Twitter (1500×500px).
4. A minimum of five reusable Pletor branded post templates exist: square post (1080×1080), landscape post (1200×630), Story/Reel (1080×1920), carousel slide, and quote card.
5. A curated app screenshot library of at least 10 annotated screenshots exists, demonstrating key features, saved in both `Distribution/netlify-site/screenshots/` and `docs/marketing/screenshots/`.
6. A feature icon set of at least 8 branded SVG icons is created, matching the existing site colour palette (`--primary: #E07A2B`).
7. Five trust badges exist in SVG and PNG (transparent background): "Local-First", "Privacy First", "Works with ProPresenter", "macOS Native", and "Human-in-the-Loop".
8. A tone of voice guide is documented covering: UK English, warm-but-professional register, church-familiar vocabulary, key differentiator messages per audience segment.
9. All assets are catalogued in `docs/marketing/brand/asset-inventory.md` with file locations, dimensions, format, and intended use cases.

## Dev Notes

### AI Prompt Templates

---

**TOOL:** ChatGPT
**PURPOSE:** Brand style guide — first draft
**PROMPT:**
```
You are a brand designer for a macOS application called Divine Link.

Divine Link is a real-time scripture detection app for churches. It listens to a live sermon, detects Bible verse references as the pastor speaks them, and lets the AV operator push those scriptures to ProPresenter (church presentation software) with a single click.

You can browse the live website at https://divinelink.netlify.app to read the current copy, feature descriptions, pricing, and visual style before writing — this will ensure the style guide reflects what already exists.

Create a comprehensive brand style guide in markdown covering:
1. Brand overview: mission ("serve the church tech community"), personality (warm, trustworthy, professional, understated), positioning (human-first, privacy-respecting, affordable)
2. Colours: Primary #E07A2B (amber orange), Background #141428 (deep navy), Surface #1e1e3a, Text #f0f0f5, Muted #9ca3af, Mercy tier #10B981, Love tier #8B5CF6
3. Typography: Cinzel for headings/display — weight 600/700/800. DM Sans for body — weight 400/500/600.
4. Tone of voice: UK English only. Warm but professional. Church-familiar vocabulary (congregation, AV operator, worship service, presentation slides). Not overly technical. Never dismissive of existing manual workflows.
5. Logo usage rules: dark backgrounds preferred, minimum clear space, never stretch or recolour
6. Audience-specific messaging for: AV Volunteers, Worship/Production Directors, Pastors, Church Administrators
7. Don'ts list: no tech jargon without explanation, no making fun of old workflows, no overpromising accuracy

Output as a clean, structured markdown document.
```
**POST-PROCESSING:** Review against `Distribution/netlify-site/index.html` colour variables. Add any Divine Link-specific product facts. Translate any American English to UK English.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Markdown document saved to `docs/marketing/brand/brand-style-guide.md`

**TOOL:** Pletor
**PURPOSE:** Social media profile image
**PROMPT:**
```
Design a social media profile image for an app called "Divine Link".
- Canvas: 400×400px, circular safe zone (320px diameter centred)
- Background: deep navy (#141428)
- Icon: stylised chain-link or infinity symbol in warm amber/orange (#E07A2B) — minimal, bold, modern
- Optional: subtle glow or halo effect around the icon in orange
- No text (profile images appear too small for legible text)
- Clean, immediately readable as a small thumbnail
- Feels: trustworthy, modern, not religious clip-art
```
**POST-PROCESSING:** Test at 40×40px to check readability. Export as PNG, 400×400px.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** PNG, 400×400px, transparent-safe or solid dark background
