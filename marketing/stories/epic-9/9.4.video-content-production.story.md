# Story 9.4: Video Content Production

## Status
Draft

## Story

**As a** Divine Link marketing lead,
**I want** a library of professional video content produced using AI video tools,
**so that** Divine Link has compelling visual media for YouTube, Instagram Reels, TikTok, and the website — without requiring a professional film crew or studio budget.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. One hero product demo video (60–90 seconds) exists, showing Divine Link detecting and pushing a Bible verse during a realistic sermon scenario. Suitable for YouTube and the website homepage.
2. Five short-form social clips (15–30 seconds each) exist, each showcasing a single feature or moment, suitable for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.
3. Three feature spotlight videos (20–40 seconds each) exist, each focused on one key differentiator: Human-in-the-Loop, Local-First Privacy, and the Panic Button.
4. Two problem/solution dramatisation videos (30–60 seconds each) exist: one showing the problem (missed/wrong verse on screen), one showing the solution (Divine Link preventing it).
5. All videos have written scripts saved in `docs/marketing/video/scripts/`.
6. All videos have corresponding thumbnails designed in Pletor, saved in `docs/marketing/video/thumbnails/`.
7. A video asset inventory exists at `docs/marketing/video/video-inventory.md` listing all videos with: title, length, format, platform target, script file, and export location.
8. All video scripts use UK English and the Divine Link brand voice.
9. All exported videos are saved to `docs/marketing/video/exports/` in the correct format per target platform.

## Dev Notes

### AI Prompt Templates

---

**TOOL:** ChatGPT
**PURPOSE:** Hero product demo script (60–90 seconds)
**PROMPT:**
```
Write a 60–90 second video script for a product demo of "Divine Link" — a macOS app for church AV operators.

Divine Link listens to a live sermon via the Mac's microphone, detects when the pastor references a Bible verse (e.g. "John 3:16"), shows the detected verse in the app's UI with a confidence indicator, and lets the operator push it to ProPresenter (the presentation screen at the front of church) with one click. The operator approves every verse before it goes live — no automation mistakes.

Script structure:
- 0–10s: Hook. "Sunday morning. Pastor starts speaking. You're ready... but are you fast enough?"
- 10–20s: Problem. Describe the old manual workflow — searching, scrolling, delays.
- 20–50s: Solution reveal. Divine Link detects the verse. Shows in the app. Operator approves. Verse appears on screen. Smooth. Professional.
- 50–60/90s: CTA. "Try Divine Link free. divinelink.netlify.app"

Format: Voiceover text | On-screen action description | Time marker
UK English. Warm but confident tone. Speaks to the AV operator. No religious pressure.
```
**POST-PROCESSING:** Review timing, ensure UK English, confirm product descriptions are accurate.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Markdown with three columns: Time | Voiceover | Action. Saved as `docs/marketing/video/scripts/hero-demo.md`

---

**TOOL:** VEO3
**PURPOSE:** Church interior establishing shot for hero demo
**PROMPT:**
```
Cinematic establishing shot of a modern evangelical church interior during a Sunday morning service.
- Wide angle, looking towards the stage and large presentation screens at the front
- Congregation visible from behind — diverse, engaged, some with hands raised
- Warm ambient lighting, stage lighting on the pastor
- A large LED screen visible at the front showing a placeholder presentation slide
- Professional production quality, not stock photo feel
- 5 seconds, smooth slow pan or static wide shot
- Aspect ratio: 16:9, 1080p minimum
```
**POST-PROCESSING:** Check: does it look like a real modern church service? Is the screen visible and prominent? Are there any logos or text that need to be removed?
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** MP4, 16:9, 1080p, 5 seconds

---

**TOOL:** VEO3
**PURPOSE:** AV operator at Mac running Divine Link
**PROMPT:**
```
Close-up shot of a church AV operator sitting at a Mac laptop in the sound/tech area of a church.
- Operator is looking intently at the screen, fingers ready on keyboard/trackpad
- On the Mac screen, visible: a dark-themed macOS app UI showing a scripture detection interface — text field showing transcription, a verse card showing "John 3:16" with a confidence bar, a glowing "Push to ProPresenter" button
- Ambient sound of a sermon audible in background (no specific words, just tone)
- Lighting: slightly dim, focused on the screen glow on the operator's face
- Age: 20s–40s, casual but professional appearance
- 5–8 seconds, static or very subtle zoom-in
```
**POST-PROCESSING:** Verify app UI looks realistic and readable. Ensure no copyrighted content is visible.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** MP4, 16:9, 1080p, 5–8 seconds

---

**TOOL:** ChatGPT
**PURPOSE:** Short-form clip scripts (batch)
**PROMPT:**
```
Write 5 short-form video scripts (15–30 seconds each) for Divine Link — a macOS app that detects Bible verses in real-time during church sermons and lets the AV operator push them to ProPresenter with one click.

Format each script as: Hook (3s) | Action/Demo (15–20s) | CTA (3–5s)

Scripts needed:
1. "Verse detected in 2 seconds" — show detection speed, verse appears almost instantly after pastor speaks
2. "One click to push" — show the simple workflow: detect → review → push. That's it.
3. "Panic button" — show a scenario where wrong content is on screen and operator hits panic button to clear it instantly
4. "Your sermons never leave your Mac" — local-first privacy: no internet needed, no cloud upload
5. "New volunteer, first Sunday" — relatability: nervous new AV volunteer, Divine Link makes it manageable

Use UK English. Upbeat but grounded. Each script ends with: "Download free at divinelink.netlify.app"
```
**POST-PROCESSING:** Review for timing accuracy, UK English, and tone.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** 5 separate markdown files, one per script

### Key Messaging to Reinforce in All Videos

2. Local-First: "No internet required, no cloud uploads"

### UK English Note
All scripts must use British English: "recognise", "behaviour", "licence" (noun), "colour", "prioritise". Avoid American church idioms — prefer "service" over "worship experience", "congregation" over "audience".
