# Story 9.3: Social Media Content Engine

## Status
Draft

## Story

**As a** Divine Link marketing lead,
**I want** a fully planned content calendar and a bank of ready-to-post social media content across all target platforms,
**so that** Divine Link can begin publishing consistently from day one without scrambling to create content each week.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. All seven target social media accounts are set up and branded (profile image, banner, bio, link) using assets from Story 9.1: YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, and Reddit.
2. A 30-day content calendar exists in `docs/marketing/social/content-calendar.md`, with each day's post planned by platform, content pillar, and format.
3. A minimum of 50 ready-to-post content pieces exist, covering all six content pillars: Problem/Solution, Feature Spotlight, Behind the Scenes, Educational, Social Proof, and Competitive Edge.
4. Each content piece includes: caption copy (UK English), relevant hashtags, a visual brief or attached Pletor graphic, and the target platform(s).
5. A platform-specific formatting guide exists in `docs/marketing/social/platform-guide.md` covering character limits, optimal image dimensions, video length guidelines, and best posting times for each platform.
6. A hashtag strategy document exists in `docs/marketing/social/hashtag-strategy.md` with 20+ researched hashtags per platform, grouped by reach (niche, mid-tier, broad).
7. A minimum of 10 engagement response templates exist for common interactions: questions about pricing, questions about ProPresenter compatibility, praise comments, comparison questions, and DM enquiries.
8. The content calendar is structured as a repeatable 30-day template that can be rolled forward each month with minimal re-planning.
9. At least 5 pieces of content per pillar are ready before the first post goes live.

### AI Prompt Templates

---

**TOOL:** ChatGPT
**PURPOSE:** Platform bio copy for all accounts
**PROMPT:**
```
Write social media bios for an app called "Divine Link" — a real-time scripture detection app for church AV operators. It listens to a live sermon, detects Bible verse references, and lets the operator push them to ProPresenter with one click. Local-first (your data never leaves your Mac), affordable, and native macOS.

You can browse the live website at https://divinelink.netlify.app to read the current feature list, pricing tiers, and positioning copy before writing.

Write bios for these platforms with their character limits:
- YouTube (about section, 1000 chars): informative, include keywords, mention ProPresenter integration
- Instagram (150 chars): punchy, include emoji-style formatting, link in bio
- Facebook (255 chars): friendly, community-focused
- TikTok (80 chars): short, energetic
- LinkedIn (220 chars): professional, include role context (macOS • Church AV • Scripture Detection)
- X/Twitter (160 chars): conversational, hashtag or two
- Reddit (subreddit sidebar, ~200 chars): helpful, community-focused

Use UK English. Warm, professional tone. No jargon.
```
**POST-PROCESSING:** Review each bio, ensure link to divinelink.netlify.app is noted, confirm UK English.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Formatted markdown list per platform

**PROMPT:**
```
You are a social media strategist for "Divine Link" — a macOS app for church AV operators. It detects Bible verse references in real-time during sermons and lets operators push them to ProPresenter.

You can browse the live website at https://divinelink.netlify.app for full product context — features, pricing, and tone — before planning the calendar.

Target platforms: YouTube (1–2x/week), Instagram (4–5x/week), Facebook (3–4x/week), TikTok (3–5x/week), LinkedIn (2x/week), X/Twitter (daily), Reddit (as appropriate).

Content pillars and their weekly frequencies:
- Problem/Solution: 3x/week (across platforms)
- Feature Spotlight: 2x/week
- Behind the Scenes: 1x/week
- Educational: 1x/week
- Social Proof: 1x/week
- Competitive Edge: 1x/fortnight

Create a structured 30-day content calendar as a markdown table with columns:
Day | Platform | Pillar | Format | Topic idea

Make it a repeatable template. Vary formats (post, reel, carousel, story, video, thread). Don't use the same topic twice in the first 30 days.
```
**POST-PROCESSING:** Review for variety. Ensure YouTube and LinkedIn get enough unique content. Ensure no pillar is overrepresented in the first week.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Markdown table, saved to `docs/marketing/social/content-calendar.md`

```
Write 8 social media captions for the "Problem/Solution" content pillar for Divine Link — a macOS app that solves the problem of church AV operators scrambling to find and display Bible verses during a live sermon.

For each post write:

2. Body (2–4 short paragraphs, conversational, tells the story of the problem then the solution)

Topics to cover:

3. Scrambling through search bars during a sermon
4. The post-service guilt of missed verses
5. A new volunteer's first Sunday on the screens
6. Sound check is fine but the screen operator is sweating
7. The moment a verse appears just in time
8. "We used to do this manually for 3 years"

Use UK English. Warm, relatable, not preachy. Speak to the AV operator, not the pastor.
```
