# Story 9.7: v2.0 Launch Campaign

## Status
Draft

## Story

**As a** Divine Link product owner and marketing lead,
**I want** a fully coordinated multi-channel launch campaign ready to execute when v2.0 ships,
**so that** the v2.0 release generates maximum organic awareness, trial signups, and subscription conversions — leveraging all the assets and audiences built in stories 9.1–9.6.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. A launch day social media pack is complete and ready to deploy across all seven platforms (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Reddit) — minimum 2 platform-native posts per platform.
2. A launch email sequence of 4 emails exists in the email platform, ready to activate: Teaser (T-14 days), Announcement (launch day), Feature Deep-Dive (T+3 days), and Final Push CTA (T+7 days).
3. A press release is written and saved at `docs/marketing/launch/press-release-v2.md`, suitable for sending to church tech journalists, bloggers, and podcast hosts.
4. The website homepage and relevant feature pages are updated with v2.0 messaging, screenshots, and feature highlights — ready to go live on launch day.
5. A launch hero video (90 seconds, VEO3-generated) specifically for v2.0 exists at `docs/marketing/video/exports/v2-launch-hero.mp4`.
6. A "What's New in v2.0" changelog/blog page is written and deployed on the website at `Distribution/netlify-site/releases.html` (or a new `/whats-new.html`).
7. A Product Hunt listing draft is complete at `docs/marketing/launch/product-hunt-listing.md` with: tagline, description, first comment, and gallery images.
8. A pre-launch tease content series (4 weeks of countdown content) is created and scheduled: at minimum 2 teaser posts per week across primary platforms.
9. All launch assets are inventoried in `docs/marketing/launch/launch-checklist.md` with status (Ready / In Progress / Not Started) for each item.
10. Launch week metrics targets are documented and a measurement plan exists.

## Dev Notes

### AI Prompt Templates

---

**TOOL:** ChatGPT
**PURPOSE:** Launch hero video script (90 seconds, v2.0)
**PROMPT:**
```
Write a 90-second launch video script for Divine Link v2.0.

Divine Link is a macOS app for church AV operators that detects Bible verse references in real-time during sermons and lets operators push them to ProPresenter with one click.

You can browse the live website at https://divinelink.netlify.app for current product copy, feature descriptions, and pricing — use this as your source of truth before writing the script.

v2.0 new features to highlight (use only those confirmed shipped):
- Modern YouVersion-inspired UI redesign
- Manual scripture search (type a reference to find and push any verse)
- Service session management (track and review what was displayed each service)
- Export and share (share service scripture lists with the congregation)
- (If ready) Multi-platform: EasyWorship and FreeShow support

Script structure:
- 0–10s: Wow opener — show the new v2.0 UI in its full beauty
- 10–25s: Brief problem recap — "We built v1 to solve a real problem. But you asked for more."
- 25–70s: v2.0 features in action — show each new feature with 8–12 seconds each
- 70–85s: "Free to start. Upgrade when you're ready. For churches that care about getting scripture right."
- 85–90s: CTA — website URL, download prompt

Format: Time marker | Voiceover | On-screen action
UK English. Confident, proud, humble. Not hype. Speaks to AV operators who trust the v1 product.
```
