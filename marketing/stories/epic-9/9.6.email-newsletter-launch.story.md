# Story 9.6: Email & Newsletter Launch

## Status
Draft

## Story

**As a** Divine Link marketing lead,
**I want** an email capture system and a ready-to-send welcome sequence on the website,
**so that** visitors who are interested but not ready to download are captured, nurtured over time, and converted into paying subscribers or vocal advocates.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. An email capture form is live on the website with at least two entry points: an exit-intent popup and a footer signup form on `Distribution/netlify-site/index.html`.
2. The email platform account is set up (Buttondown or Mailchimp free tier), verified, and connected to the website capture form(s).
3. An automated welcome email sequence of exactly 5 emails is created in the platform, set to send over 14 days:
   - Email 1 (Day 0): Welcome + quick start guide
   - Email 2 (Day 3): The problem — "The scripture scramble every church faces"
   - Email 3 (Day 7): How Divine Link works (60-second overview)
   - Email 4 (Day 10): Social proof — use cases and testimonials
   - Email 5 (Day 14): CTA — "Try it free today" + pricing breakdown
4. All 5 email bodies are written, edited for UK English, and loaded into the platform.
5. A monthly newsletter template is created in the platform with: branded header graphic, intro section, feature/update section, community tip section, and footer with unsubscribe link.
6. A segmentation strategy is documented in `docs/marketing/email/segmentation-strategy.md` with at least three audience segments and tailored messaging notes for each.
7. All forms, emails, and opt-in mechanisms include GDPR-compliant consent language and an unsubscribe mechanism that works correctly.
8. A test email send of each sequence email has been reviewed and confirmed rendering correctly in both mobile and desktop email clients.
9. Email capture and open rate metrics are being tracked via the platform dashboard.

## Dev Notes

### Recommended Email Platform

**Buttondown** (buttondown.email) — Recommended for this project:
- Free up to 100 subscribers
- Markdown-native (easier to write and maintain)
- Clean, privacy-focused
- Good for indie developers and single-person operations
- GDPR compliant infrastructure
- Simple API for form integration

**Alternative: Mailchimp** — if automation segmentation is a priority:
- Free up to 500 contacts
- Better visual email builder
- More complex to set up but more powerful

Write a welcome email for new subscribers to Divine Link — a macOS app that detects Bible verse references in real-time during sermons and lets AV operators push them to ProPresenter with one click.

### Website Implementation Notes

**Footer Form (inline in `index.html`):**
- Add before the closing `</footer>` tag
- Form action: platform embed code (Buttondown or Mailchimp)
- Minimal design: single input, button, GDPR one-liner below
- Style consistently with existing footer dark theme

**Exit-Intent Popup:**
- Trigger: `mouseleave` event on `document` when `clientY <= 10px` (cursor approaching top/tab bar)
- Only fire if user has been on page for >30 seconds
- `sessionStorage` key `exitIntentShown` prevents re-triggering in same session
- Overlay: semi-transparent dark backdrop, centred modal card in `--bg-card` colour
- Dismiss: clicking backdrop OR "No thanks" link dismisses and sets `sessionStorage` flag

**GDPR Consent Language:**
```
By subscribing, you agree to receive occasional emails about Divine Link updates and church tech tips.
We'll never share your email. Unsubscribe any time.
```
