# Story 9.2: Website Landing Page Revamp

## Status
Approved

## Story

**As a** church AV operator or worship tech decision-maker visiting divinelink.netlify.app,
**I want** to land on a visually polished, credibility-building, and informative landing page,
**so that** I immediately understand what Divine Link does, trust the product, and feel compelled to download or subscribe.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Typography is replaced from Inter to Cinzel (display/headings) + DM Sans (body), loaded via Google Fonts.
2. All emoji icons in feature cards, why-choose cards, and the integration bar are replaced with inline SVGs (Lucide or Heroicons).
3. Scroll-triggered entrance animations are added to section headers, feature cards, pricing cards, why-choose cards, steps, and the CTA block — respecting `prefers-reduced-motion`.
4. A functional mobile hamburger navigation menu is added; `.nav-links` is no longer just hidden on mobile.
5. The hero right-column displays a real app screenshot (`02-hero-verse-detected.png`) styled as a macOS window mockup with proper shadow.
6. A testimonials section is inserted between `#how-it-works` and `#pricing` with at least a placeholder structure for 2–3 church quotes.
7. The "Why Choose" card 3 (Better Value) layout is fixed — the `.highlight-stat` element no longer breaks the visual rhythm of the grid.
8. The CTA section includes a reassurance line ("Free to start · No credit card required · macOS 14+") and a secondary "View Pricing" link alongside the download button.
9. The footer is expanded to a 3-column structure (brand column, product links column, legal/support column).
10. A subtle SVG noise texture overlay is applied to the page body as a fixed `::after` pseudo-element at ~2.5% opacity.
11. The integration bar logos are replaced with styled pill badges (icon + text) rather than raw emoji strings.
12. The `step::after` connector lines are hidden at ≤1024px breakpoint to fix the 2-column layout misalignment.
13. All changes are confined to `Distribution/netlify-site/index.html`. No new files, no build tools, no framework changes.
14. The page passes a manual browser check in Chrome and Safari (latest) and looks correct at 375px, 768px, and 1280px viewports.

### Typography Notes
Cinzel is a serifed display font based on classical Roman inscriptions. It will render noticeably larger than Inter at the same font-size. The `h1` at `3.5rem` should be reduced to `3.2rem` for visual parity. Section titles (`2.8rem`) may need reduction to `2.5rem` — check visually.

**Google Fonts URL to use:**
```
https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Cinzel:wght@600;700;800&family=DM+Sans:opsz,wght@9..40,400;9..40,500;9..40,600&display=swap
```
